# Voiceover scripts

About 30 seconds each. Speak calmly, like you're telling a friend, not selling. Leave a short breath between lines.

## Guide version (fieldprint-launch)

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 0 to 5s | You took the shot. Your guests still talk about it. |
| 5 to 10s | Now they can hang it on their wall. |
| 10 to 16s | Fieldprint gives you your own print shop. Your best photos, one link. |
| 16 to 21s | Guests order. We print it, frame it, and ship it anywhere in the world. |
| 21 to 26s | You set the price. You get paid. No stock, no packing. |
| 26 to 30s | Fieldprint. Sell prints of your safari photos. Link in bio. |

## Buyer version (fieldprint-buyer)

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 0 to 5s | Back from safari? Remember this? |
| 5 to 10s | Now you can hang it at home. |
| 10 to 16s | Your guide has their own print shop, full of photos from your trip. |
| 16 to 21s | Pick a size and a frame, see exactly what gets printed, and we deliver it to your door. |
| 21 to 26s | And every print supports your guide. |
| 26 to 30s | Fieldprint. Ask your guide for their link. |

## Recording tips (phone is fine)

- Quiet room with soft things around (a wardrobe full of clothes is ideal). Avoid echoey kitchens and bathrooms.
- Phone about 20 cm from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- Record each line on its own, two or three takes. Easier to fix than one long take.
- Use the Voice Memos or Voice Recorder app, then send the files over.
